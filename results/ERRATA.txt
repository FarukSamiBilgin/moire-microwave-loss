ERRATA for m30_loss.json and m31_route_ranking.json
====================================================

These two JSON files are project artifacts kept unchanged in this record.
Some of their descriptive and bibliographic fields predate the reference,
claim and arithmetic checks behind the preprint. Where a field below
differs from the preprint text or from p4_yeniden_uretim.py, the preprint
and the independent script are correct. Fields are given as JSON paths.

1. Generating scripts (included in this record)
   - m30_loss.json: run_m30_loss.py
   - m31_route_ranking.json: run_m31_route_ranking.py
   Both scripts are deterministic and need only NumPy; run from a folder
   laid out as scripts/ and out/, each regenerates its JSON file with
   identical content (checked 2026-11-13). The descriptive text fields
   listed below are written by these scripts, so the scripts carry the
   same outdated wording. p4_yeniden_uretim.py recomputes,
   without reading the JSON files, every number the preprint uses except
   the readout-chain figures (80 dB attenuation, residual thermal occupancy
   5.17e-3 at 5.5 GHz, acquisition budget 4.19 h), which are taken from
   m30_loss.json (chain.*) and were not recomputed independently.

2. Author list (McRae et al., Rev. Sci. Instrum. 91, 091101 (2020))
   - literature.fit_methods.loss_review
   - experiment.observables[0].ref
   The author list in these fields is wrong; the correct list is the one
   in the preprint's bibliography.

3. Reading of the participation curve
   - participation.participation_curve_reading
   The thresholds written there (p = 0.5 at omega L/Z0 = 1.4; 0.99 above
   ~80; 5% agreement below ~0.35) are not those of the exact expression.
   Correct values (preprint Sec. II, Table I): p = 0.5 at 0.46, 0.99 at
   5.7, 0.997 at 10.5; the perturbative form is within 5% only up to ~0.3.

4. Quarter-wave length
   - design.ladder_note, headline
   "~5.8 mm" quarter wave: the preprint uses 5.4 mm at 5.5 GHz (7.1 mm at
   4.2 GHz) at the same effective permittivity.

5. Q_i of NbSe2 device D6 (Zaman et al.)
   - literature.zaman_nbse2_KI.devices[5][8]
   - participation.published_vdw_ladder.rows[5].Qi
   28000 is the arXiv-version value; the published value used in the
   preprint is 2.0e4.

6. Amplifier attributions and the HEMT-only estimate
   - literature.amplifier.ref, literature.amplifier.name
   - chain.bill_of_materials.amplifier.preferred
   - choices[4].basis, choices[9].basis, choices[9].down
   - comparison_with_m15.m15.parametric_amplifier
   - chain.bill_of_materials.amplifier.baseline
   The JTWPA and KI-TWPA attributions in these fields did not pass the
   reference check and are not used in the preprint. The 4.19 h budget
   (chain.campaign) was computed at n_sys = 3 quanta; the "about four days"
   HEMT-only figure is inconsistent with it and is not used. The preprint
   states only that the signal-limited part grows roughly fourfold with a
   HEMT alone.

7. Contact-width label
   - contacts.contact_ceiling[2].label, choices[6].up
   "ASSUMED 2 um" contact width: the width of the published 3.2 kOhm
   contact is not available. The preprint applies the 3.2 kOhm unchanged at
   the 10 um pad (rho_c = 16000 Ohm um) and states the assumption.

8. Fabrication-recipe attributions
   - experiment.fabrication[0] .. experiment.fabrication[4]
   The 250 nm Nb base layer, the graphite gate option, the tear-and-stack /
   argon-glovebox / 1.10 +/- 0.05 deg recipe attributed to Tanaka et al.,
   and the NbN contact option are not supported by the checked sources.
   The preprint's fabrication paragraph gives the recipe with its sources.

9. Other fields that did not pass the checks and are not used
   - design.linearity_check.conservative_alternative
     (the "6 nA" junction critical current attributed to Jha et al.)
   - literature.fit_methods.photon_number, chain.drive_power.formula
     ("Eq. 5"; the preprint cites Supplementary Eq. (3) of Zaman et al.)
   - experiment.observables[2] (dome-edge percolation prediction)
   - literature.hBN.eps_ref ("de Put"; the author is Van de Put)
   - literature.graphene_qubit.note
